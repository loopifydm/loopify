META ADS

Meta Ads for local businesses: a practical starting framework

A local campaign works best when the offer, creative, audience and follow-up are designed together.

Offer

Give the audience a clear reason to respond: consultation, trial, scholarship, quote, site visit or another relevant next step.

Audience

Start with a practical geographic and customer profile, then use campaign data to refine targeting.

Creative

Test multiple hooks and formats. The first creative is a hypothesis, not the final answer.

Budget

Choose a test budget you can sustain long enough to learn. Avoid changing everything every day.

Tracking

Track enquiries and qualified outcomes, not only clicks or impressions. Connect website, WhatsApp and sales follow-up where possible.
