LEAD GENERATION

From social media views to qualified enquiries

Views are useful only when they create attention that can move toward trust and action.

1. Create repeatable content pillars

Teach, demonstrate expertise, answer objections and show proof. Consistency creates familiarity.

2. Give attention a destination

Use a clear offer and one next step rather than sending every visitor to a generic homepage.

3. Reduce friction

Short forms, WhatsApp, clear CTAs and fast follow-up can make the journey easier.

4. Qualify leads

Ask only the questions needed to understand fit, intent and urgency.

5. Close the measurement loop

Feed sales outcomes back into marketing so future campaigns optimise toward lead quality rather than volume alone.
