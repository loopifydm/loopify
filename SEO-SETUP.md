## GA4
1. Create a GA4 web data stream for the Loopify website.
2. Copy the Measurement ID (`G-XXXXXXXXXX`).
3. Open `tracking.js`.
4. Replace `G-XXXXXXXXXX` in `LOOPIFY_GA4_ID` with the real ID.
5. Commit and redeploy.

## Meta Pixel
1. Create a Meta Pixel in Events Manager.
2. Copy the Pixel ID.
3. Open `tracking.js`.
4. Replace the placeholder value in `LOOPIFY_META_PIXEL_ID`.
5. Commit and redeploy.

## Google Search Console
Verify the domain/property in Google Search Console, then submit:
`https://loopifydm.github.io/loopify/sitemap.xml`

## Google Business Profile
Create/claim the Loopify Business Profile in Google Business Profile, complete the business category, service areas, phone, website and photos, then request verification. Use the exact real business information; do not publish a guessed street address.

## Important
Testimonials and performance numbers are intentionally not fabricated. Replace the clearly marked testimonial placeholders only with permissioned, verified client feedback and real campaign results.
